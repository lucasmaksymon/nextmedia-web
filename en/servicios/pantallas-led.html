FAQ

What advantage does an LED screen have over a traditional billboard?

It allows moving pieces, creative changes with no printing or installation cost, and dayparting: your message can differ in the morning and at night.

Can I change the creative during the campaign?

Yes. Since they're digital media, the piece updates remotely within hours, with no additional production costs.

What file formats do I need?

We send you the exact specs for each screen (resolution, duration and file size) and, if you need it, we help adapt the pieces.
